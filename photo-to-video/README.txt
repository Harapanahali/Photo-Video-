Photo to Video
1. Open this folder in VS Code
2. Right-click index.html -> Open with Live Server (use Chrome or Edge)
3. Add photos (or "Use a sample photo"), pick a motion, press Play
4. Press "Record video", wait for it to finish, then download

Files: index.html, css/style.css, js/motions.js, js/render.js, js/recorder.js, js/app.js
